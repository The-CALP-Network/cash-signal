CALP Network Cash Signal cash-related indicators across humanitarian responses

Cash Signal is a living snapshot of cash and voucher assistance across our fifteen priority responses: funding, plan requirements, modality preference, coordination capacity, pulled from FTS and other sources into one place. It tells us where cash is winning, where it’s stalled, and where the sector should focus, and it’s honest about what we don’t know.
